Starkheben 5×5 Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Short version: the Android app doesn't collect, send or share any data. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

Android app

  • Your workouts, weights, schedule and settings are stored only on your device, inside the app.
  • The app has no internet permission, no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • The only permission it uses is vibration, for the rest timer.
  • When you use Export data, the app saves a file to a place you choose. What happens to that file is up to you. Import data reads only a file you pick.
  • Android may include the app's data in your own device backup (for example your Google account backup), as with any app. You control this in your phone's settings.
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes all its data.

Web version (optional)

A separate web version of the app can be used with an account (a username and a password). If you create an account there, your username, a hashed password and the training data you enter are stored in a database so you can use them on several devices. Other people signed in to the same web app can see your username, working weights and training history. Nothing from the web version is used for ads or shared with third parties. To have your account and data deleted, contact us using the link below.

Children

The app isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
